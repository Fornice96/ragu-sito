Our story

Two Neapolitans, one idea of what Italian food should taste like.

Ragù opened at 5–7 Guildhall Street on 15 March 2026, brought to Preston by chef Luca Scognamiglio and Vincenzo Gaglione — both from Naples, both tired of "Italian" restaurants that aren't. Luca runs the kitchen the way he learned it at home; Vincenzo runs the room. Between the two of them, the whole idea comes down to three words: consistency, quality, community.

Preston noticed. The Lancashire Post covered the opening, and Blog Preston came back a few weeks later for a full review — the smoked salmon fettuccine and the carbonara came in for particular praise. You'll find both on the à la carte menu below.

The dining room

A room built around the table, not around itself.

No white tablecloths, no fuss — just warm light, close tables and the pass in full view, the way a proper Neapolitan trattoria should feel.

It's a small room by design: easy to fill for a Tuesday lunch, easy to book out for a birthday. Either way, it's Luca and Vincenzo running it, not a rota of strangers.

Preston

Five minutes from the Flag Market, part of the neighbourhood.

Guildhall Street sits right in the city centre — an easy stop before the market, after work, or on a Sunday walk. Ragù was built to be a regular's spot as much as a destination: the same faces at the bar, the same table saved for a Friday night.
