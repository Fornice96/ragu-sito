Our story

Two Neapolitans, one idea of what Italian food should taste like.

Ragù opened at 5–7 Guildhall Street on 15 March 2026, brought to Preston by chef Luca Scognamiglio and Vincenzo Gaglione — both from Naples, both tired of "Italian" restaurants that aren't. Luca runs the kitchen the way he learned it at home; Vincenzo runs the room. Between the two of them, the whole idea comes down to three words: consistency, quality, community.

Preston noticed. The Lancashire Post covered the opening, and Blog Preston came back a few weeks later for a full review — the smoked salmon fettuccine and the carbonara came in for particular praise. You'll find both on the à la carte menu below.
